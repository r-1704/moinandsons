What types of products do you offer?

We produce fitted sheets (Jersey, Molton, Terry, Polyester, Jacquard), towels (bath and kitchen), and napkins. For more information, contact us at info@moinandsonstextile.com .

Can you manufacture products from our own designs?

Yes! We specialize in turning your designs into finished textile products, from yarn or fabric to the final stitched product. For more details, email us at info@moinandsonstextile.com .

Do you offer custom printing on fabrics?

Yes, we provide high-quality printed fabrics according to your design requirements. For more info, write to info@moinandsonstextile.com .

How do you ensure product quality?

Our skilled team uses modern machinery and strict quality control from yarn selection to finished products. For detailed information, email info@moinandsonstextile.com .

Can you handle bulk orders?

Absolutely! We efficiently manage large-scale orders while maintaining consistent quality. For more details, reach out to info@moinandsonstextile.com . .
